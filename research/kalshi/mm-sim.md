Research only. One-contract quotes, replaced at each snapshot. Quotes are pulled before close.

Net P&L is cash plus final inventory at the last observed mid, after fees. Spread captured measures quote improvement versus the posting mid. Signed adverse-selection loss measures fill price against the first observed mid at least 15 minutes later; negative values are favorable. This diagnostic is not subtracted from net P&L again. Unmarked fills lack a later observation. Realized P&L is closed inventory profit less all incurred fees. Unrealized P&L is the remaining inventory marked against average entry cost. No settlement or liquidation is simulated.
